● Android · built for UPI

Your bank SMS, turned into a clean expense log.

Ledgerly reads payment SMS on your phone, pre-sorts each one into the category you used before, and asks you to review them once. No manual entry, no uploading your messages.

Three steps, one review

Most payments are sorted before you even open the app.

1

Scan

Ledgerly finds payment SMS and ignores OTPs, promos, recharge offers and failed payments.

2

Pre-sort

Known payees get their category automatically, learned from what you confirmed before.

3

Review once

Glance at the sorted list, fix anything wrong, and confirm the whole batch in one tap.

A look inside

Swipe between tabs with your finger, like any modern app.

Your SMS never leave your phone.

All reading and sorting happens on the device. Only the amount, payee, date, category and your note are saved to your account, protected by per-user Row Level Security. The SMS text is never uploaded.

  • ✓ Parsed on-device
  • ✓ OTPs and promos are skipped
  • ✓ Every row private to your account

Built with

A TypeScript parser with its own test suite, a Supabase backend and a native feel.
